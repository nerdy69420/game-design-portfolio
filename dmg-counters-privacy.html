DMG Counters — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Short version: DMG Counters doesn't have accounts and doesn't collect any personal information itself. Your game data stays on your device. The app shows banner ads via Google AdMob and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove them and unlock extra features, both of which are described below. The only other network calls the app makes are to the public pokemontcg.io API to look up and download Pokémon card data and artwork.

Who we are

DMG Counters is an independent, fan-made damage-counter tracker for the Pokémon Trading Card Game, built and published by an individual developer (Sandsware). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or specifically approved by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company. Pokémon and Pokémon character names are trademarks of their respective owners; card data and images retrieved through the app come from the third-party pokemontcg.io API and remain the property of their respective rights holders.

Information we collect

We — the app's developer — do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves. Specifically:

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or user profile of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own. The app does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, crash-reporting services, or any other tracking or telemetry beyond the advertising SDK described below.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The app shows a banner ad via Google AdMob. See "Advertising" below for what that involves.
  • In-app purchases (Google Play Billing). The app offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads and unlock extra features, processed entirely by Google Play. See "In-app purchases" below.
  • No location, camera, contacts, or microphone access. The app doesn't request or use any of these permissions. Uploading a custom battlefield background (a purchased feature) uses Android's built-in photo picker, which shares only the one image you pick — the app never gets broader gallery or camera access.

Advertising

DMG Counters shows a banner ad at the top of the screen via Google AdMob, unless you've purchased the Pro unlock. AdMob's SDK may collect and use an advertising identifier and other technical/device information to serve and measure ads, which is not something we — the app's developer — receive or store ourselves; it's handled entirely by Google. Review Google's ad policy and Google Privacy Policy for details, including how to opt out of personalized advertising via your device's ad settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads).

In-app purchases

The app offers a single optional one-time purchase ("Pro") that removes ads and unlocks a custom damage/heal counter tray, custom battlefield backgrounds, and saved teams. This purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details, and no account with us is created or required. Google's own privacy policy governs the purchase transaction itself.

What's stored on your device

All app data lives in a local database on your device and never leaves it, except where described in "Network requests" below. This includes:

  • Your current game (active/bench Pokémon, damage counters, status conditions, turn count).
  • Card sets you've chosen to download for offline search.
  • App preferences (theme, sound volume, battlefield background choice).
  • A short list of recently used cards, to speed up repeat searches.
  • If purchased, your custom damage counters and any saved teams of cards you've created.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. We have no server, so we have no copy of it and no way to recover it.

Network requests

When you search for a card online, or download a card set for offline use, the app sends a request directly from your device to the public pokemontcg.io API — a third-party service we do not operate — to fetch card data and card artwork. These requests include the search terms you type (e.g. a Pokémon's name) and, inherently to any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to that service in the same way it would be to any website you visit. We do not receive, log, or have access to this traffic ourselves. Review pokemontcg.io's own policies for how they handle requests made to their API.

If you only use cards you've already downloaded for offline search, or don't use the search feature at all, the app makes no network requests.

Children's privacy

DMG Counters is not directed at children and is not primarily intended for a child audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since we don't collect personal information from any user ourselves. Ads shown through Google AdMob are not configured for child-directed treatment; if you believe a child is using this app, please supervise their use accordingly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync or analytics — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
